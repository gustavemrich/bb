Rules

1. Submit a name

One name per bid. Keep it something that can actually be said out loud in public.

2. Attach a number

Your bid is the amount you're pledging. Bids are public and show on the leaderboard.

3. Highest bid wins

When the countdown hits zero, the top bid at that moment takes the name.

4. Winner gets the invite

The winning bidder gets access to the private party celebrating the name selection.

5. Bad-faith bids get pulled

Slurs, impersonation, and joke bids nobody intends to honour are removed without notice.

6. Final say

Any bid can be rejected for any reason. Nothing here is a binding offer or a contract.
